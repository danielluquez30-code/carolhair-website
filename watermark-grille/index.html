Our story

Two brothers. One neighborhood table.

Watermark Grille is owned and run by two brothers who learned a strong work ethic from their father. Watermark is our sixth business venture — and the one we're proudest of.

We set out to build a casual, relaxed alternative to the national chains: honest steaks, fresh fish, generous pastas, good wine and a bar where everyone's welcome. Our success is measured by hard work and effort, but most of all by more than two decades of satisfied guests.
